# Economic Analysis Report
## Egypt Macroeconomic Indicators (2014–2024)

### 1. Executive Summary

This report examines the development of Egypt's macroeconomic performance between 2014 and 2024, focusing on three key indicators: GDP growth, inflation, and unemployment.

The analysis uses annual data from the World Bank's World Development Indicators database and applies Python-based statistical analysis and data visualization techniques.

The findings highlight significant fluctuations in economic growth and inflation, alongside a general improvement in labour market conditions over the study period.

### 2. Economic Growth Analysis

Egypt's GDP growth rate averaged approximately 4.21% during the period under review.

The highest growth rate was recorded in 2022, reaching 6.59%, while the lowest rate occurred in 2024 at approximately 2.40%.

These fluctuations indicate changes in economic activity and highlight the importance of examining growth performance alongside price stability and labour market developments.

### 3. Inflation Analysis

Inflation averaged approximately 15.78% over the study period.

The highest inflation rate was recorded in 2023, reaching 33.88%, followed by 28.27% in 2024.

These figures highlight the substantial inflationary pressures affecting the Egyptian economy during the later years of the study period.

High inflation can weaken household purchasing power and increase business operating costs, potentially affecting consumption and investment decisions.

### 4. Unemployment Analysis

The unemployment rate averaged approximately 9.42% between 2014 and 2024.

The rate declined from 13.11% in 2014 to 6.55% in 2022, indicating a significant improvement in labour market conditions.

However, unemployment increased slightly to 6.82% in 2024, suggesting that labour market improvements were not entirely sustained.

### 5. Correlation Analysis

The correlation analysis revealed the following relationships:

- GDP Growth and Inflation: -0.19
- GDP Growth and Unemployment: -0.11
- Inflation and Unemployment: -0.16

These results indicate weak negative statistical correlations between the selected indicators.

However, these relationships should be interpreted cautiously, as correlation does not establish causation. Other economic factors may influence these indicators.

### 6. Key Economic Findings

The analysis highlights three main findings:

1. Economic growth experienced considerable fluctuations, with a slowdown in 2024.
2. Inflationary pressures intensified significantly during 2023 and remained elevated in 2024.
3. Unemployment declined substantially compared with 2014, although a slight increase was observed in 2023 and 2024.

Overall, the findings suggest that improvements in labour market conditions do not necessarily translate into stronger economic growth or price stability.

### 7. Conclusion

Egypt's macroeconomic performance between 2014 and 2024 reflects the interaction of several economic challenges.

While unemployment declined considerably over the period, economic growth remained subject to fluctuations, and inflation emerged as a major challenge, particularly during 2023 and 2024.

The analysis demonstrates the importance of examining macroeconomic indicators jointly to obtain a more comprehensive understanding of economic performance and support evidence-based policy assessment.

### 8. Data Sources and Limitations

**Source:** World Bank – World Development Indicators (WDI).

**Period:** 2014–2024.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

**Limitations:** The analysis is based on 11 annual observations. Correlation analysis identifies statistical associations but does not establish causal relationships. The findings should therefore be interpreted within the scope of the selected indicators and period.
